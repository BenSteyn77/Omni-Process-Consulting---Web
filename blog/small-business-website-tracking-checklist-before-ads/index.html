The Small Business Website Tracking Checklist Before Running Ads

Do not buy more traffic until the website can prove what happened to the traffic you already paid for.

Google Ads Readiness9 min readUpdated September 2026

The short version: Before running ads, a small business website should have analytics installed, conversion events tested, call and form tracking working, a retargeting pixel where relevant, mobile pages checked, thank-you pages or confirmation events in place, and a follow-up process connected to the CRM or inbox. If those basics are missing, ads may create clicks without usable attribution.

Why This Checklist Matters

Most struggling ad accounts do not fail only because the ads are bad. They fail because the website and follow-up system cannot prove what happened after the click.

The business owner sees spend leaving the account, a few enquiries coming in, and no clear answer on which campaign, keyword, ad, or page created the result. That makes every next decision slower and more emotional.

This checklist is built for local service businesses that are entering Google Ads, already running ads without clear results, or considering a new paid traffic push.

1. Analytics Installed On Every Important Page

Confirm that Google Analytics or another analytics platform is installed across the homepage, service pages, landing pages, blog pages, contact page, and offer pages.

Do not assume the homepage tag covers the whole website. Static HTML sites, custom landing pages, and older WordPress builds often have missing tags on deeper pages.

For implementation help, start with our guide on installing Google Analytics on a small business website.

2. Lead Events Are Defined

A lead event is the action that tells you a visitor did something valuable. Pageviews are not enough.

  • Contact form submitted
  • Phone number clicked
  • Booking button clicked
  • Quote request started
  • Checkout completed
  • Emergency call button tapped

If the business sells a paid audit or package through Stripe, the completion event after payment matters more than the form itself. The form may only appear after purchase, so tracking must reflect the real funnel.

3. Google Ads Conversion Tracking Is Ready

Google Ads needs conversion data to judge which clicks are valuable. Google's own Ads help lists several conversion tracking options, including website actions, calls, apps, and imported conversions.

At minimum, a small business running lead generation ads should track form submissions and phone calls. Better still, connect qualified lead stages in the CRM so the account learns from better signals over time.

This is where the future marketing app idea fits: a simple reporting layer can help the owner see which campaign, page, and lead action created value without digging through three dashboards.

4. Meta Pixel Or Retargeting Pixel Is Installed If Meta Will Be Used

If Facebook or Instagram traffic is part of the plan, the Meta Pixel should be installed before traffic starts. Retargeting is much weaker when the audience starts collecting late.

This matters for local service businesses because many visitors do not convert on the first visit. They compare, ask a spouse, get distracted, or wait until the problem becomes urgent.

The follow-up article to read here is why retargeting matters for local service businesses.

5. Mobile Experience Is Actually Tested

Most local service leads happen on mobile. Check the website on a real phone, not only in a desktop preview.

  • Phone buttons must be easy to tap
  • Forms must be short enough to complete
  • Sticky headers must not cover content
  • Page speed must not feel painful on mobile data
  • The main call to action must appear before the visitor gives up

If the mobile version is awkward, ads will expose that weakness quickly.

6. Follow-Up Is Connected

A tracked lead still needs to be answered. For contractors and local service businesses, missed calls and slow callbacks are often bigger leaks than the landing page.

Connect form notifications, missed-call text-back, after-hours answering, and CRM reminders before increasing spend. This is where CRM automation and AI receptionists can protect the money already being spent on traffic.

Quick Answers

What should a small business track before running Google Ads?

Track phone clicks, form submissions, quote requests, booking actions, and purchases or checkout completions where relevant.

Do I need a thank-you page for conversion tracking?

A thank-you page is useful, but not required if event tracking is configured correctly. The important part is that the completed lead action is recorded reliably.

Why do ads fail when the website looks good?

A website can look good but still lack analytics, conversion tracking, mobile usability, clear calls to action, and fast follow-up.

Should I fix tracking before or after launching ads?

Fix tracking before launching ads whenever possible. Otherwise you may pay for traffic without knowing which traffic created leads.
