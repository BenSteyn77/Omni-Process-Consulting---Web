Why Most HVAC Ads Waste Money

HVAC ads fail when they chase clicks instead of qualified calls.

  • Broad keywords that attract poor-fit searches
  • No separation between furnace, heating, AC, maintenance, and emergency intent
  • Weak landing pages
  • No call tracking
  • Missed calls after the click
  • No follow-up workflow

In Buffalo, this can be especially costly during heating season when urgent calls can become booked jobs quickly.
