The main limited launch offer is an AI missed-call receptionist for HVAC and local service businesses that lose revenue when calls go unanswered.

Why This Matters for HVAC and Emergency Service Companies

Many high-intent service calls happen when the team is busy, on a job, driving, closed for the day, or dealing with peak-season pressure. If the caller reaches voicemail or waits too long, they often contact another company.

Missed call automation for service businesses gives the customer a faster first response and gives the owner better information to act on.

  • Capture missed and after-hours calls
  • Reduce lost opportunities from voicemail
  • Separate urgent issues from routine inquiries
  • Send cleaner lead summaries to the right person
  • Create a better handoff into follow-up or booking
