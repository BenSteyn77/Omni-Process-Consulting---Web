The real problem

Most companies do not have one broken system. They have several small gaps working against each other.

A missed call becomes expensive when there is no text-back. Advertising becomes difficult to judge when bookings are not attributed. A CRM adds little value when the team does not use it consistently.

The assessment follows these connections so you can distinguish a visible symptom from the underlying process problem.
